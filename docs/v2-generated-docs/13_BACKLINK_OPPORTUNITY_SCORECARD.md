## Future Scoring Contract

When evidence exists, score each opportunity with separately visible fields:

1. evidence confidence;
2. source relevance and legitimacy;
3. target-page fit;
4. user/business usefulness;
5. correction, reclamation, or relationship feasibility;
6. spam/policy/reputation risk;
7. owner access or external dependency;
8. estimated effort.

Do not collapse these fields into a single score until the rubric and thresholds are reviewed against a real sample. A high tool metric must never override irrelevance, illegitimacy, or policy risk.

## Prohibited Shortcuts

- buying links or reviews;
- bulk directory submission;
- automated unsolicited outreach;
- fabricated affiliations, sponsorships, citations, or mentions;
- scripted exact-match anchor requests;
- treating tool-estimated authority as fact;
- claiming profile ownership or inconsistency without verification;
- redirecting unrelated legacy URLs to the homepage merely to retain a link.
