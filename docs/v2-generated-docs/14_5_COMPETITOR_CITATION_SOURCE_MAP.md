## Minimum Accepted Observation Row

Each future row must preserve:

- observation date/time and tester location;
- exact surface/model label shown to the tester;
- exact prompt without retroactive editing;
- brand or entity mentioned/recommended;
- every visible cited source domain and URL;
- source/page type based on the visible page, not a guessed category;
- whether the source is first party, third party, public record, directory/profile, publisher, community content, or another reviewed type;
- whether the cited claim is accurate and supported;
- screenshot, transcript, or export location;
- notes about personalization, sign-in, location, experiment, or result variability.

## Competitor Approval Contract

Before competitor interpretation, provide at least two owner-approved competitors with:

1. canonical public domain;
2. reason the business/search audience is comparable;
3. comparable service and geography notes;
4. approved prompt families;
5. permission to inspect public answer results and cited pages;
6. a consistent observation window and capture method.

A nearby HVAC company is not automatically a valid competitor. A business mentioned in one answer is not automatically a priority competitor. A cited domain is not automatically authoritative, accurate, or link-worthy.

## Future Interpretation Rules

Once evidence exists, Codex may group observed citations by:

- first-party versus third-party source;
- public record, business profile/directory, local publisher, trade/manufacturer, expert guide, community discussion, or other reviewed type;
- page purpose such as definition, comparison, service-area reference, company facts, review/reputation, or transaction;
- citation recurrence across dated observations;
- claim accuracy and agreement across sources;
- target-page fit for One Call's five indexable routes.

Do not infer preference, ranking, permanence, causality, or winning format from a single observation. Do not convert cited-source recurrence into automated outreach, copied content, artificial reviews, or bulk directory creation.
