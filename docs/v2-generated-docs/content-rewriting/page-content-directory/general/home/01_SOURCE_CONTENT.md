**[BUTTON]** My furnace just quit on a 10° night. Are you really 24/7?

**[ANSWER]** Yes, really. Call (801) 864-2045 — Jesse answers, including nights and weekends. Most emergency calls along the Wasatch Front get a same-night response. We carry the common parts (igniters, capacitors, blower motors, gas valves) on the truck so most no-heat calls are fixed on the first visit.

**[BUTTON]** How much is a new furnace or AC install?

**[ANSWER]** Depends on size, brand, and what's behind the wall. We quote installs in writing, itemized, with no surprise add-ons. Most full furnace replacements run $4,500–$8,500; full AC swaps $4,000–$7,500; full heat pump systems $9,000–$14,000 — minus federal 25C tax credits up to $2,000 and any active Utah HEEHRA rebates.

**[BUTTON]** Should I replace my old furnace with a heat pump?

**[ANSWER]** For most northern Utah homes — yes, a cold-climate heat pump is a great move. They handle Utah winters down to single digits, qualify for federal tax credits, and lower summer cooling costs. We size it carefully so you don't over-buy or under-buy.

**[BUTTON]** Do you charge a diagnostic fee?

**[ANSWER]** $89 flat diagnostic fee. Waived if you book the repair with us. Gold Members pay $0. We'll never tell you your unit is done without showing you exactly why.

**[BUTTON]** What's a maintenance plan and is it worth it?

**[ANSWER]** Our Gold Membership is $99/year and includes two free seasonal tune-ups, priority scheduling, $0 diagnostic fees, 15% off parts and labor, and a tech who already knows your system.

**[BUTTON]** Do you do mini-splits / ductless?

**[ANSWER]** Yes — ductless mini-splits are great for additions, garages, basements, shop spaces, and older homes without ductwork. We install single- and multi-zone systems.
