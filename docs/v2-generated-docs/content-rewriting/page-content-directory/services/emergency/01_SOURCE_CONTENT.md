When to call

### [H2] Urgent HVAC problems deserve a direct answer.

#### [H3] No heat

The furnace or heat pump is running without warm air, cycling off, or not starting during cold weather.

#### [H3] No cooling

The air conditioner is not running, is blowing warm air, or cannot keep the property at a safe temperature.

#### [H3] Electrical or equipment warning

A breaker repeatedly trips, the system makes a new harsh noise, or there is a burning smell near the equipment.

#### [H3] Active leak or shutdown

Water is collecting around HVAC equipment, a condensate line is overflowing, or the system has shut itself down.
