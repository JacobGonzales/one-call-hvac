### [H2] Know What You're Approving.

A useful HVAC recommendation should connect the finding, the work, the price, and the next step. Use these questions before approving a repair or replacement.

#### [H3] What was tested?

Ask which component or system was checked, what was observed, and which finding supports the recommendation.

#### [H3] Repair or replacement?

Have the repair scope and replacement scope explained separately so you can compare what each option addresses.

#### [H3] What is included?

Confirm equipment, labor, disposal, permit or warranty responsibilities, and anything excluded from the written quote.

#### [H3] What happens next?

Confirm the schedule, access needs, primary contact, and the walkthrough or findings you should expect when work is complete.

#### [H3] Details to gather before you call

- Identify the equipment involved if known: furnace, central AC, heat pump, mini-split, thermostat, or ductwork.

- Write down what the system is doing, when it started, and whether the problem is constant or intermittent.

- Record any thermostat message, fault code, unusual sound, or unusual odor without taking equipment apart.

- Confirm the property address, best contact information, and any access details that may affect the visit.

- Keep the approval questions nearby: what was tested, what is recommended, what is included, and what happens next.
